Our services, in detail

Four services, taken separately or together, scoped and quoted in writing before anything begins.

Program 01 · Where the time goes

Operations review

We spend time watching the work rather than reading the process document, which is usually out of date. The output is a written set of findings with the two or three changes that would make the largest difference, ranked by effort rather than by ambition.

  • 2 to 6 weeks
  • Small and mid-sized organisations
  • Quoted in writing
  • Access to your teams

Program 02 · Changed, then written down

Process improvement

A change nobody documented reverts within a quarter. We redesign the process with the people who run it, write it down in language they recognise, and stay long enough to see whether it holds under a normal week rather than a quiet one.

  • 4 to 12 weeks
  • Operational teams
  • Quoted in writing
  • Time with the team

Start this program

Program 03 · Who decides what

Structure & responsibilities

Growing organisations accumulate overlapping responsibilities, and the cost shows up as delay and duplicated work rather than as an argument. We map who decides what, where handovers happen, and what nobody owns — then propose a structure that fits the organisation's actual size.

  • 4 to 8 weeks
  • Growing organisations
  • Quoted in writing
  • Access to managers

Program 04 · Numbers that get used

Planning & reporting

Reporting fails when there are forty measures and no agreement on what any of them mean. We pick the few that drive decisions, define them so the same question gets the same answer next quarter, and set up a monthly review that takes an hour and changes something.

  • 4 to 8 weeks
  • Management teams
  • Quoted in writing
  • Access to your data
