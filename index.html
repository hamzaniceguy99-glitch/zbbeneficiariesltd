Frequently asked

The questions we get most

Do you give financial or investment advice?

No. We provide management consultancy other than financial management, and we're not FCA authorised. Financial, tax and legal questions go to regulated professionals.

How long is a typical engagement?

A review is usually two to six weeks; an implementation project runs longer. Both are scoped and priced in writing before anything starts.

Will we just get a report?

Only if that's what you asked for. Our preference is to implement one change, document it and see it hold — reports that sit in a drawer help nobody.

How much of our people's time do you need?

Some, unavoidably — the work can't be observed from outside. We agree the time commitment in the scope so it isn't a surprise.

Do you work with small organisations?

Yes, that's most of our work. Small organisations feel process problems faster and can change direction more quickly.

What does it cost?

It depends on scope. Everything is quoted in writing after a free first meeting, and nothing is charged before you accept.
